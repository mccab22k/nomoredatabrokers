No More Data Brokers

A practical checklist for finding data-broker records, submitting removal requests, and remembering when to check again. Your identifying information stays on your device.

Working principle Your data should not cost money to remove.

No account · no backend · no tracking

Removal progress
Statuses and dates are stored locally. Confirmed removals automatically become due for re-check after each broker's interval.
